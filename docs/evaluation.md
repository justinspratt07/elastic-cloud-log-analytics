# SaaS Evaluation

## Potential advantages

- Reduces direct responsibility for platform patching and infrastructure care.
- Provides managed availability and centralized access to Elastic tooling.
- Allows technical staff to spend more time on analysis and service delivery.
- Simplifies access to Kibana dashboards for operational review.

## Tradeoffs

- Recurring subscription and data-ingestion costs must be evaluated.
- Migration planning is required for indexes, dashboards, users, and retention.
- Data governance and regulatory requirements must be verified before adoption.
- Internet connectivity and vendor-service availability become dependencies.

## Production decision criteria

A production recommendation should compare total cost of ownership, data volume,
retention requirements, recovery objectives, access controls, compliance needs,
staffing effort, and migration risk. The proof of concept supports feasibility;
it does not replace a complete production assessment.
